# Reynolds American

Reynolds American Inc. is the second-largest tobacco company in the United States and a subsidiary of British American Tobacco (BAT). Its subsidiaries include R.J. Reynolds Tobacco Company (Camel, Newport, Pall Mall), American Snuff Company, Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company (Natural American Spirit), and R.J. Reynolds Vapor Company.

**Website:** https://www.reynolds-american.com  
**Type:** company (consumer goods / tobacco)  
**Fortune 500:** Yes  
**Parent:** British American Tobacco (BAT)
